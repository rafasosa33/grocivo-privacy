Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grocivo ("Grocivo", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Grocivo Android application (the "App"). Grocivo helps you create and manage grocery shopping lists.

We aim to keep your shopping lists on your device and to be transparent about the limited information that may be handled by third-party advertising services.

1. Information You Enter in the App

You can use Grocivo without creating an account or registering. Information you add to your shopping lists, such as item names, quantities, categories, and other list details, is stored locally on your Android device.

We do not operate a Grocivo account system or our own server for storing or syncing your shopping lists. The list information you enter is not sent to Grocivo servers by the App.

2. Information Collected by Grocivo

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, or precise location through the App's grocery-list features. Grocivo does not use a separate analytics service to track how you use the App.

The App displays advertising using a third-party advertising provider. That provider's software may collect or receive certain information from your device, as described in Section 5. The provider's collection is separate from the local storage of your grocery lists.

3. Local Storage and Your Shopping Lists

Your grocery lists are stored on your device so that you can access and manage them in the App. We do not receive or maintain copies of those lists on our own servers.

You can remove list items or lists using the features available in the App. App data may also be removed through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App. The availability of any device backup or restoration is controlled by Android and your device settings, not by Grocivo.

4. Permissions

Grocivo is designed to manage shopping lists locally. If a version of the App requests an Android permission, that permission will be used for the feature explained at the time it is requested. You can review or revoke permissions in your Android settings.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Grocivo displays advertisements provided by a third-party advertising service. Depending on the provider, its configuration, your region, and your device settings, the advertising SDK may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers or other device identifiers;
  • IP address and general device or network information;
  • App interaction and advertising event information, such as ad views or clicks;
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address, where used by the provider;
  • Diagnostic or fraud-prevention information.

The advertising provider may use this information to deliver, limit, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements, and to detect invalid activity or fraud, subject to its own privacy policy and applicable law. We do not use a separate analytics service, but an advertising provider may process ad-related events for advertising measurement and reporting.

We have not identified a specific advertising provider in this policy. The provider's name and privacy-policy link should be added here before publication once the advertising SDK used in the App has been confirmed. Please review that provider's documentation and SDK data practices.

6. How Information Is Shared

Grocivo does not sell your shopping lists or the personal information you enter into them. Your locally stored list content is not shared by Grocivo with advertising providers.

Information collected by the advertising SDK may be transmitted directly to the advertising provider and its partners as needed to provide the advertising service. Those parties handle information under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Shopping-list information remains in the App's local storage on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Grocivo does not keep a server-side copy of your lists.

Advertising-related information is retained by the advertising provider according to that provider's retention practices and policies. Please consult the provider's privacy policy for details about its retention and deletion procedures.

8. Data Security

Because shopping-list information is stored locally, its security also depends on the security of your device, its operating system, and your device settings. We recommend using a device screen lock and keeping Android and the App up to date. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Grocivo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require account registration. Advertising services, if shown, must be configured and used in accordance with applicable laws and the advertising provider's requirements for the audience and age group targeted by the App.

10. Your Choices

You can control your use of Grocivo and certain device-level data by:

  • Editing or deleting shopping lists within the App;
  • Clearing Grocivo's app data or uninstalling the App;
  • Reviewing advertising and privacy controls available in Android and your Google account, where applicable;
  • Contacting us with questions about this Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Please review this page periodically.
